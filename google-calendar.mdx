WhiteWhale connects to your Google Calendar and automatically prepares a research brief before any meeting with a tracked account. No manual prep needed — the brief lands in your inbox 24 hours before the call with everything you need to walk in prepared.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the left nav and scroll to **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Google Calendar and click Connect">
    Follow the prompts to authorize WhiteWhale to access your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your mode">
    Select how WhiteWhale should handle your upcoming meetings:

    | Mode | What it does |
    | --- | --- |
    | **On** | Sends a research brief 24 hours before each meeting with a tracked account. Nothing is added to your account list automatically. |
    | **On — Auto Upload** | Sends research briefs and automatically adds any account you have a meeting with to your WhiteWhale list. Uses account credits. |
    | **Off** | Pauses the integration. No briefs are generated. Your connection is preserved. |
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure which mode to pick? Start with **On**. You get pre-meeting briefs without touching your account list or credits. Switch to Auto Upload later if you want your calendar to drive your book of business automatically.
</Tip>

## What's in the brief

WhiteWhale sends the brief by email 24 hours before the meeting. Each brief includes:

- Meeting time, company HQ location, and employee count
- **What they do** — a plain-English summary of the company
- **Why Now** — the signal story: what's changed recently and why now is the right time to be talking to them

## Disconnecting

To disconnect, go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Google Calendar** → click **Disconnect**. You can reconnect at any time.
