## Use Claude to build your signal list

The fastest way to build a great signal list is to use the WhiteWhale Signal Creator — a Claude skill trained specifically on how WhiteWhale signals work, what makes them fire reliably, and how to write the non-obvious signals that actually move deals.

Tell it what you sell, who you sell to, and what triggers a purchase. It will generate a prioritized signal set with explanations for each one.

<Card title="Download the WhiteWhale Signal Creator Skill" icon="robot" href="https://getwhitewhale.com/whitewhale-signal-creator.skill.zip" cta="Download">
  A Claude skill that builds a custom signal list for your ICP. Works in Claude.ai and Claude Desktop.
</Card>

**How to use it:**

1. Download the `.skill` file above
2. Open [Claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and upload the file, or install it in Claude Desktop
3. Tell Claude what you sell, who buys it, and what your best customers looked like before they bought
4. Claude will generate a prioritized, ready-to-paste signal set tailored to your business

<Tip>
  The skill is trained to find the non-obvious signals — the ones in job postings, website language, and company behavior that reveal pain without ever mentioning your product category. These are the signals that drive the most pipeline.
</Tip>

## Using the Signal Library in WhiteWhale

The fastest way to find signal ideas is directly inside the platform. WhiteWhale reads your business and generates suggestions tailored to your ICP — no blank page, no guessing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Signal panel">
    From the Signals page, click **Add Signal** in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Signal Library">
    Switch to the **Signal Library** tab. WhiteWhale will generate a list of signal ideas based on your business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a signal">
    Browse the suggestions and click into any one you want to add. For example: _Do they use Outreach?_ or _Are they hiring an SDR manager?_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a rank and add it">
    Assign a priority — **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** — and click **Add Signal**. It joins your list and starts scanning immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure where to start? The in-platform library is the best first stop. Come back to the templates below to fill gaps or go deeper on a specific category.
</Tip>

## Signal writing frameworks

Use these when the starter pack doesn't fit your ICP or you want to build something more specific.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Simple Grammar Builders">
    The fastest way to construct a valid signal:

    - Does {account} mention `<topic / tech / initiative>`?
    - Is {account} hiring `<roles / functions>`?
    - Has {account} announced `<event / change>`?
    - Is {account} expanding into `<market / region>`?
    - Is {account} consolidating or replacing `<tool / category>`?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Persona → Pain → Trigger">
    Match a buyer persona to a pain point and a triggering event.

    | Persona | Pain | Trigger |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Sales | Pipeline growth | New CRO |
    | Marketing | Enablement | Hiring burst |
    | IT | Modernization | Platform migration |
    | Security | Compliance | Regulation or audit |

    **Example:** Does {account} have a new CISO and mention security modernization or compliance gaps?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Buying Stage Ladder">
    Build one signal per buying stage to cover the full funnel.

    | Stage | What to look for | Example signal |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Awareness | Mentions the problem |  |
    | Consideration | Hiring, programs, budgets |  |
    | Decision | RFPs, pilots, platform choices |  |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Change-Event → Outcome">
    Pair a change event with its intended outcome for higher-precision signals.

    **Pattern:** Has {account} `<change_event>` to achieve `<desired_outcome>`?

    **Example:** Has {account} centralized GTM operations to accelerate revenue growth?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Time & Geography Qualifiers">
    Add these directly in the Context Window to tighten any signal.

    | Qualifier type | Example context |
    | --- | --- |
    | Region | _US, Canada, UK, and EU only_ |
    | Recency | _Prioritize results from the last 90 days_ |
    | Seniority | _Leadership announcements and manager-level roles and above only_ |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
