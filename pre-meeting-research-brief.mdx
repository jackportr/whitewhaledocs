WhiteWhale connects to your Google Calendar and automatically prepares a research brief before any meeting with a tracked account. No manual prep needed — the brief lands in your inbox 24 hours before the call.

## What's in the brief

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 06 10 At 2 46 53 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-06-10-at-2.46.53-PM.png)
</Frame>

Every brief includes:

- **Meeting time, HQ location, and company size** at a glance
- **What they do** — a plain-English summary of the company and what they build
- **Why Now** — the signal story: what's changed recently, what they're trying to achieve, and why now is the right time to be in front of them

## How to connect Google Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    In the left nav, go to **Settings** → **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Google Calendar and click Connect">
    Follow the prompts to authorize WhiteWhale to access your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how WhiteWhale should handle your meetings">
    You'll be asked to select one of three modes:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="On — Research briefs only (recommended for most users)">
        WhiteWhale analyzes your upcoming meetings and sends a research brief 24 hours before each meeting with a tracked account. Your account list stays as-is — nothing is added automatically.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="On — Auto Upload (most automated)">
        WhiteWhale sends research briefs **and** automatically adds any account you have a meeting with to your WhiteWhale list. Good for reps who want their calendar to drive their account list. Keep in mind this will use account credits.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Off">
        Calendar integration is paused. No briefs will be generated and no accounts will be added. Your connection is preserved so you can turn it back on anytime.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure which mode to pick? Start with **On** (research briefs only). You get the full value of pre-meeting prep without touching your account list or credits. Switch to Auto Upload later if you want your calendar to automatically populate your book of business.
</Tip>

## Disconnecting

To disconnect Google Calendar, go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Google Calendar** and click **Disconnect**. You can reconnect at any time.
