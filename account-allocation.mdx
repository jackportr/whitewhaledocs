## Reassigning accounts already added

Once accounts are in your active list, you can reassign ownership to any team member directly from the Accounts page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to All Accounts">
    Switch from **My Accounts** to **All Accounts** in the top filter to see your full team's book of business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the account to reassign">
    Browse or search for the account you want to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the owner">
    Click the **Owner** field on the account row and select a different team member from the dropdown. The change takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If your CRM is connected, ownership changes in WhiteWhale will sync to your CRM automatically.
</Info>
