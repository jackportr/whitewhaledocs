If a signal isn't returning what you expected, you don't need to delete it and start over. Most signal issues fall into one of three categories — and each has a straightforward fix.

## The three common signal problems

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Wrong geography" icon="location-dot">
    Results are coming from countries or regions you don't serve.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Too broad" icon="arrows-left-right">
    The signal is matching accounts that technically fit the question but aren't what you meant.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Results are stale" icon="clock">
    The signal is surfacing old matches from months or years ago.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fix 1: Wrong geography

Use the **Context Window** to tell the system which countries or regions to restrict results to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the signal">
    From the Signals page, locate the signal returning results from the wrong location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Context Window">
    Click the **book icon** next to the signal. This opens the Context Window where you can give the system additional instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your geography constraint">
    Type your restriction in plain language. For example:

    - _Job listings from the United States only_
    - _Headquarters in the US, UK, or Canada_
    - _Exclude results outside North America_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. WhiteWhale will re-run the signal with the new constraint applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 2: Signal is too broad

When a signal is casting too wide a net, you have two options depending on how much you need to change.

**For small edits** — use the inline signal editor. Click directly into the signal text and add the missing specificity. For example, changing _"Are they running an account-based marketing program?"_ to _"Are they running an email-based ABM program?"_ is a quick edit that takes seconds.

**For bigger changes** — use the Context Window. If you need to add multiple conditions or explain nuance the signal text alone can't capture, open the book icon and write out the full context. For example: _Only match if the ABM program is focused on email outreach, not paid ads or direct mail._

<Tip>
  The core principle: just tell the system what you want. The more specific context you give it, the more precisely it will match.
</Tip>

## Fix 3: Results are stale

When you upload an account, WhiteWhale looks back **12 months** for historical signals by default, so you get the full recent picture on day one. To only see more recent activity, edit the signal and add a time limit.

Open the signal editor or Context Window and add language like:

- _Only in the last 3 months_
- _Only in the last 6 months_

Once you save, WhiteWhale will re-run the signal and update both your account scores and your Account Suggestions with the fresh results.

### How recent are new signals?

The 12-month lookback only applies to historical signals when an account is first uploaded. After that, WhiteWhale checks your monitored accounts on an ongoing basis, so new signals are recent. Depending on your plan, a new signal shows up with at most a bi-daily, weekly or monthly delay.

<Note>
  The **Signal Recency** filter on the Accounts page only changes which accounts you see. It doesn't change what WhiteWhale looks for. To change what a signal detects, edit the signal.
</Note>

## When a signal result is wrong

Occasionally WhiteWhale won't get the right information for an account. No signal system is perfect. If you see a result that's wrong, flag it to the WhiteWhale team at [hello@getwhitewhale.com](mailto:hello@getwhitewhale.com) so we can keep improving.

In most cases you can fix it yourself. Add clarification to the signal so it's clearer what you're looking for, using the fixes above. For example, _Only count a first sales hire if it's the company's first-ever sales role, not a replacement._

## Two ways to edit a signal

| Method | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit the signal text** | Small, targeted word changes |
| **Context Window (book icon)** | Geography constraints, multiple conditions, time limits, or anything requiring explanation |

Both methods trigger a full re-scan of your accounts when saved.
