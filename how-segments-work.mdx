<Info>
  **A segment is a saved set of filters.** Instead of rebuilding the same filter combination every time, you save it once and come back to it with one click.
</Info>

## What you can filter by

When building a segment, you can combine any of these filters:

- **Signal activity** — accounts that have a specific signal, or any signal in a given time range
- **Deal status** — in CRM, not in CRM, specific deal stage
- **Owner** — filter to one rep or the whole team
- **Company size** — employee count range
- **Location** — specific states, countries, or global
- **Industry** — one or more industries
- **Account status** — active, suggested, or both

Mix and match any combination. The accounts that match all your filters are what appear in the segment.

## Segment ideas to get you started

| Segment name | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| **White Whales** | Enterprise accounts you're actively hunting |
| **Founding roles** | Companies under 50 employees hiring founding AE, SDR, or GTM roles |
| **Closed lost \+ new signal** | Accounts you lost that now have fresh signal activity |
| **Mid-market** | Accounts in your 50–500 employee range |
| **New signals this week** | Any account with signal activity in the last 7 days |
| **Not in CRM** | Accounts WhiteWhale is tracking that haven't entered your pipeline yet |

## How to create a segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Accounts page and set your filters">
    Use the filter bar at the top to build the combination you want. You'll see the matching accounts update in real time as you add filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save as Segment">
    Once your filters look right, click **Save as Segment** and give it a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access it any time">
    The segment appears in your segments list in the left nav. Click it to instantly reload those filters and see the current matching accounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Segments are live — they always show the accounts that match your filters right now, not a static snapshot from when you saved them. A closed-lost segment with a new signal filter will automatically surface new matches as they appear.
</Tip>
