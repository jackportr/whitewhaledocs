In your search results, you'll see two types of accounts — monitored accounts (already active) and suggestions shown in orange (found by WhiteWhale but not yet tracked). Activating a suggestion turns it into a monitored account with full signal data, Why Now summaries, and live updates.

## How to activate suggestions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the accounts you want to activate">
    Use the checkboxes to select individual suggestions or select all at once. WhiteWhale will show you how many of your selection are suggestions vs. already monitored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Activate">
    You'll be prompted to choose how to assign them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an assignment method">
    - **Assign to one person** — all selected accounts go to a single team member
    - **Round-robin** — accounts are evenly split across your team
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once activated, the accounts switch from suggestions to monitored. This means they get live updates, new signals, and full Why Now summaries — all the details that were locked behind the preview unlock.

<Tip>
  Try to activate as many suggestions as your plan allows. Monitored accounts get live signal updates every day — suggestions don't. The sooner you activate, the sooner you know when something changes.
</Tip>
