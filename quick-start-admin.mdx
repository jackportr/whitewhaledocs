This guide walks you through everything you need to do as an admin to get WhiteWhale fully configured for your team. Follow these steps in order — each one builds on the last.

---

## Step 1: Invite your team

Add everyone who will be using WhiteWhale before you start assigning accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Team page">
    Click **Team** in the left nav and click **Add User**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each team member">
    Enter their name, email, and select a role:

    - **Super Admin** — sales leadership, RevOps, anyone who needs full visibility
    - **Member** — individual AEs and SDRs working their own territory
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Invite">
    WhiteWhale emails them their login automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Build your signal list

Signals are the questions WhiteWhale asks about every account. A good signal list is the foundation of everything — account scores, live searches, and the Why Now summaries all depend on it.

**Aim for 10–15 signals.** Mark your top 5–8 as High priority.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Signals page">
    Click **Signals** in the left nav, then click **Add Signal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want to find">
    Type what you're looking for in plain English. WhiteWhale will convert it into the right format. You don't need to phrase it as a question — just describe it.

    Not sure what signals to add? Start here:

    - Did they hire a new CRO or VP of Sales?
    - Are they hiring multiple AEs or SDRs at once?
    - Did they announce new funding?
    - Are they expanding into a new market or geography?
    - Do they mention a problem your product solves?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set signal priority">
    - **High** — your strongest buying signals. Use for 5–8 signals.
    - **Normal** — standard weight signals.
    - **Low** — background context, mute these so they don't flood your queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the signal and repeat">
    Build up to 10–15 signals before moving on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Add accounts or run a search

You have two options here — use one or **both**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Option A: Upload a list of accounts">
    Use this if you already have a set of accounts you want to monitor — assigned territories, closed-lost deals, a target list from your CRM.

    Go to the **Accounts** page → click **New Search** → select **Upload a List**. Name the list, paste your company domains, and assign an owner. WhiteWhale cleans up the formatting automatically.

    If your list is larger than your credit limit, toggle on **Clean the List** to filter by signals and firmographics before uploading — so only the best accounts use your credits.

    <Card title="Adding Accounts" icon="plus" href="/adding-accounts">
      Full guide including the Clean the List feature for large lists.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Option B: Run a live search to find new accounts (Recommended)">
    Use this if you want WhiteWhale to find net-new accounts that match your ICP and already have signal activity.

    Go to the **Accounts** page → click **New Search** → select **Find New Accounts**. Name the search, then set your filters:

    - **Signals** — leave as Any Match (avoid Must Have for now)
    - **Time range** — how recent signals need to be
    - **Company size** — employee count range for your ICP
    - **Industry** — match to LinkedIn industry categories
    - **Location** — countries or US states you serve

    Click **Save and Make Live Search**. Results often appear within minutes and grow daily as the search runs.

    <Card title="Setting Up Account Suggestions" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/setting-up-account-suggestions">
      Full setup guide with filter recommendations.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  You can run both at the same time. Many teams upload a known list of accounts and run a live search to find net-new ones in parallel.
</Info>

## Step 4: Connect your integrations

Get WhiteWhale data flowing into the tools your team already uses.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CRM — HubSpot or Salesforce">
    Go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → find HubSpot or Salesforce and click **Set Up**. Use the one-click setup for a standard configuration.

    Once connected, WhiteWhale scores, signals, and Why Now summaries sync to your CRM daily.

  <Accordion title="Slack or Microsoft Teams">

    Have each rep go to **Settings** → **User Notifications** and turn on at minimum **Daily Signals** and **Assigned Accounts**.

## Step 5: Assign accounts to your team

Once accounts are in WhiteWhale — either uploaded directly or found by a live search — assign them to your reps.

**For accounts already in your list:** Click the **Owner** name on any account and select the rep it should go to.

**For accounts from a search:** Select the suggestions (shown in orange) using the checkboxes → click **Activate** → choose to assign to one person or round-robin across the team.

**Pro tip:** Filter by geography, industry, or employee count before assigning to quickly route accounts to the right rep by territory or segment.
