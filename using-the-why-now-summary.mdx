## What the Why Now tells you

Click into any account and you'll see three things: the Why Now summary, the underlying signals that informed it, and the sources those signals came from.

The Why Now pulls from all active signals on the account — the facts, quotes, numbers, and dates — and synthesizes them into a coherent story. A good Why Now tells you what's changing at the company, why that creates urgency, and what outcome they're likely trying to achieve.

**In about two seconds, you should know whether this account is worth your time.**

## How to use it

**Step 1: Read the Why Now first.** Before digging into individual signals, read the summary. If the story doesn't connect to what you sell, move on. If it does, go deeper.

**Step 2: Dig into the signals.** The signals beneath the summary are the raw evidence — the job postings, press releases, earnings quotes, and website changes that built the story. Use these to get specific details you can reference in outreach.

**Step 3: Build your angle with AI.** Once you know the story, use an AI tool to help you turn it into a pitch. Copy the Why Now and relevant signal details into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini and ask it to help you connect the account's situation to what you sell.

For example:

> _"Here's what's happening at this account: \[paste Why Now\]. We sell \[product\] that helps companies \[outcome\]. Help me write a two-sentence opener that connects their situation to our value prop."_

This is how the best teams on WhiteWhale are turning signal data into pipeline — not by sending generic outreach, but by leading with the specific story happening at the account right now.

<Tip>
  The Why Now is only as strong as your signals. If the summary feels generic or off, it's usually a sign that your signals need to be more specific to your ICP. See [How to Get Unique Signal Ideas](/how-to-get-unique-signal-ideas).
</Tip>
