## What's included

WhiteWhale includes unlimited users, unlimited live searches, and all integrations on every plan. The one thing credits control is **actively monitored accounts** — the accounts WhiteWhale scans every day for new signals.

## How credits work

Your plan comes with a set number of account credits. Each credit represents one actively monitored account for the month.

**You use a credit when you:**

- Upload an account via New Search → Upload a List
- Activate a suggestion from a live search

Once an account is monitored, WhiteWhale scans it daily and gives you the full signal data — sources, quotes, facts, and the Why Now summary. Suggestions (shown in orange) are just a preview until activated.

## Checking your credit usage

Go to **New Search** → **Upload Your List**. The credit bar will appear showing:

- Your total credit limit for the plan
- How many accounts you're currently monitoring
- How many more you can add this month

<Info>
  **Each month your credit balance resets** and you can choose to swap out the accounts you are currently monitoring up to your plan limit. For example, on a 300-account plan you can archive your current accounts and monitor a fresh 300 each month.
</Info>

## Adding more credits

If you need more account capacity:

- From **Settings** → scroll to **License** → click **Manage License**
