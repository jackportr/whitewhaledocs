## What's included

WhiteWhale includes unlimited users, unlimited searches, and all integrations on every plan. The one thing credits control is **monitored accounts** — the accounts WhiteWhale scans every day for new signals.

## How credits work

Your plan comes with a set number of account credits. Each credit represents one monitored account for the month.

**You use a credit when you:**

- Upload an account via New Search → Upload a List
- Activate a suggestion from a Find New Accounts search

Once an account is monitored, WhiteWhale scans it daily and gives you the full signal data — sources, quotes, facts, and the Why Now summary. Suggestions (shown in orange) are just a preview until activated.

## Checking your credit usage

Go to **New Search** → **Upload a List**. The credit bar will appear showing:

- Your total credit limit for the plan
- How many accounts you're currently monitoring
- How many more you can add this month
- How many accounts you can archive to free up space

## When archiving frees a credit

Your credit balance resets every month, and that reset controls when an archived account gives its credit back.

- **Before your first monthly reset:** archiving an account frees its credit at the next reset, not straight away.
- **After your first monthly reset:** any account you archive frees its credit immediately.

Not sure how many credits you can free right now? Go to **New Search** → **Upload a List**. It tells you how many accounts you can archive to free up space.

<Info>
  Each month you can swap out the accounts you are monitoring, up to your plan limit. For example, on a 300-account plan you can archive your current accounts and monitor a fresh 300 each month.
</Info>

## Adding more credits

If you need more account capacity:

- From **Settings** → scroll to **License** → click **Manage License**

## Invoices and receipts

Invoices are emailed automatically to your billing email address.

You can also find past invoices in **Settings** → **Manage License**. Manage License is only available to the billing email owner. To see your invoices there, log in to WhiteWhale with the billing email address.

<Tip>
  If you only see an option to upgrade under Manage License, you're probably logged in with a different account from the billing email. Log in with the billing email address, or ask the person who owns it.
</Tip>
