## What's included

WhiteWhale includes unlimited users, unlimited Account Suggestions, and all integrations on every plan. The one thing credits control is **actively monitored accounts** — the accounts WhiteWhale scans every day for new signals.

## How credits work

Your plan comes with a set number of account credits. Each credit represents one actively monitored account for the month.

**You use a credit when you:**

- Add an account directly from the Accounts page
- Activate an account from Account Suggestions

Once an account is active, WhiteWhale monitors it daily and gives you the full signal data — sources, quotes, facts, and the Why Now summary. Accounts sitting in Account Suggestions are just a preview until activated.

## Checking your credit usage

Click **Add Accounts** in the top right of the Accounts page. You'll see:

- Your total credit limit for the plan
- How many accounts you're currently monitoring
- How many more you can add this month

## Adding more credits

If you need more account capacity, there are two ways to upgrade:

- From the **Add Accounts** window → click **Add Credits**
- From **Settings** → scroll to **License** → click **Manage License**
