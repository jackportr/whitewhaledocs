WhiteWhale can send signal alerts, account suggestions, and deal updates directly into your Microsoft Teams channels — so your reps get notified where they already work.

<Info>
  **Teams alerts are available on Enterprise plans.** To get set up, contact the WhiteWhale team at [hello@getwhitewhale.com](mailto:hello@getwhitewhale.com) and they'll walk you through the configuration.
</Info>

## What you'll receive in Teams

Once connected, WhiteWhale delivers the same alerts as Slack — directly into the channel of your choice:

- **Daily Signals** — new signal activity on your accounts, with the source, key details, and a link back to the platform or your CRM
- **Assigned Accounts** — when a new account is assigned to a rep
- **Recommended Accounts** — when Account Suggestions surfaces new matches

## Getting set up

Teams setup is handled by the WhiteWhale team. Reach out and they'll get your workspace connected.

<Card title="Contact WhiteWhale" icon="envelope" href="mailto:hello@getwhitewhale.com">
  Email [hello@getwhitewhale.com](mailto:hello@getwhitewhale.com) to get Teams alerts configured for your account.
</Card>

## After setup: turn on your notifications

Once your workspace is connected, each rep should go to **Settings** → **User Notifications** and enable the alerts they want to receive in Teams.

At minimum, turn on **Daily Signals** and **Assigned Accounts** to get full value from the integration.
