## How to add accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Search on the Accounts page">
    From the Accounts page, click **New Search** in the top nav.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your list">
    Give the list a name — something that describes what these accounts are. For example: _Jack's Territory_, _Closed Lost Q1_, _Enterprise Targets_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your domains">
    Paste in company domains in any format — line by line, comma separated, straight from a CSV. WhiteWhale cleans them up automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an owner">
    Select who these accounts should be assigned to. You can assign them to yourself or to anyone else on the team — useful if you're uploading lists for other reps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tags (optional)">
    Tag the batch to keep things organized. Examples: _Enterprise_, _Working_, _Closed Lost_, _Reached Out_. Tags make it easy to filter and build segments later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upload">
    WhiteWhale starts scanning immediately. Accounts that already exist in the system will appear right away. The rest will populate as they finish scraping — signals, details, and the Why Now will show up as they're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you have a big list, you can also just find the best accounts.

If you have a large list of 100, 1,000 or more accounts and you're not sure every account fits your ICP, you can filter it before it goes live.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Clean the List">
    Before clicking Upload, toggle on the **Clean the List** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your signal requirements">
    Select which signals an account must have before it's uploaded. Only accounts that match at least one of your selected signals will be added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set firmographic filters (optional)">
    Add company size parameters or other firmographic filters to further narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    WhiteWhale scans the full list, applies your filters, and only uploads the accounts that pass. The rest are filtered out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Clean the List is useful when you have more accounts than credits, or when you're uploading a raw CRM export and want WhiteWhale to surface only the accounts with real signal activity. Let the platform do the filtering instead of sorting by hand.
</Tip>

## A third way: Account Suggestions

WhiteWhale can also recommend net-new accounts you haven't thought of yet based on your signals and ICP. See [How Account Suggestions Work](/how-account-suggestions-work) to set that up.
