## Your accounts are already sorted

When you land on the **All Accounts** page, accounts are sorted by most recent signal date by default. The accounts at the top have the freshest activity — start there.

Click into any account and the first card will show you the most recent signal with its source, details, and Why Now.

## Filter by time range

If you want to narrow the view, go to **Filters** → **Signal Recency** and set a window — for example, last 3 months. This shows you only accounts with signal activity in that period.

## Export for outbound campaigns

Once you've filtered your accounts, click **Export** to pull the data into a CSV. Use this to run campaigns in Clay, your outreach tool, or anywhere else you work your accounts.
