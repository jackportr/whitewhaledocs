## Signals are custom to your sales motion

Unlike generic intent data (job changes, funding rounds), every signal in WhiteWhale is specific to what _you_ care about. Your signal list reflects your ICP, your qualification criteria, and your sales process — no one else's.

When you set up WhiteWhale, you'll build a list of 10–20 questions that describe your ideal buying moment. The platform monitors every account in your book against those questions, every day.

## What WhiteWhale monitors

For each signal, WhiteWhale scans a variety of live sources:

- **News articles and press releases** — funding, leadership changes, expansions, acquisitions
- **The prospect's website** — messaging shifts, new product pages, pricing changes
- **Job postings** — directly from the company's ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday) and their careers page
- **Earnings calls and earnings reports** — for public companies
- **Company social posts** — LinkedIn and other public channels

Sources are checked daily. The signal count you see next to each signal reflects how many accounts have matched it in your current book.

## How signals become alerts

When WhiteWhale finds a match, it surfaces the account in your signal queue with:

- The signal that fired
- The source it was found in
- A **Why Now** summary explaining what changed and why it matters
- A suggested message you can copy and send

From there, you can act on the account, dismiss it, or push it to your CRM — all without leaving WhiteWhale.
